    Where the numbers come from

    Players, visits, favorites and chart positions come from Roblox's public API, collected every three hours. A day's "avg online" is the average of that day's samples. Only games that appear on one of Roblox's charts (or did in the last 30 days) are tracked, so platform totals cover those games, not all of Roblox.

    Revenue is an estimate. Roblox doesn't publish what games earn. Each day's new visits are multiplied by per visit, the formula Profitable.app publishes (2% of visits spend, 150 Robux average, 70% developer share, $0.0038 per Robux). Games that sell well per player will be under-counted, and the reverse. The "Roblox earning rank" column is Roblox's own Top Earning chart, which ranks real earnings but shows no amounts.

    For history from before this tracker started, open a game and follow the links to RoTrends, RoMonitor, RoWatcher or Profitable.app.
